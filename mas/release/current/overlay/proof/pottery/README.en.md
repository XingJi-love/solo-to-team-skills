# From four draft labels to three print-ready pages

[Open the three-page print proof](labels.pdf) · [Open the returned HTML](labels.html) · [中文](README.md)

A ceramics exhibition had a signed-off register and a partly transcribed label sheet. The exhibition is fictional. The Mac operator recorded the team running in native DeepSeek Harness. At intake, the lead counted four works and noticed that the draft gave the wrong material and year for Q02. It acknowledged the files and waited for the promised change. [Register](inputs/register.csv) · [Original draft](inputs/draft.md)

In the same chat, the requester withdrew Q03 and set the wall order to Q04, Q01, Q02. A producer prepared the labels. A separate reviewer opened the actual HTML and change log, checked the source and rendered the print proof. The lead returned the HTML, the staff change log and the review in the original chat. Both input files retained their original bytes. [Amendment](change.txt) · [Returned change log in Chinese](change-log.md)

The print proof has three landscape A6 pages. Q04 and Q01 come first. Q02 is last and reads 青瓷 (celadon), 2024. Each label carries the title, credited workshop, material and year exactly as given in the register.

The operator played the requester and supplied the intake and amendment. The lead, producer and reviewer handled the work between those two messages and the final return. The PDF came from the reviewer's print check. The HTML was the deliverable returned by the lead. A later filename-only change, verified separately in a browser, protects successive downloads in the same folder.
