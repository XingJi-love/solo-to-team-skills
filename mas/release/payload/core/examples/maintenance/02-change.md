# 维修预约 · 途中变更 / Maintenance bookings · Change request

等首席识别第一张卡的底稿后，再发送本卡对应语言的正文。此题全为合成资料。

## 中文

北楼从 09:00 起至 10:00 临时封闭，10:00 整开放。四单保持原地点，保留不受影响的预约。

请生产席另存新版，制作一张可直接执行的调度表，列明每个工单的起止时间、地点以及原预约保留或变动的原因。附上受影响站点的通知草稿，只写草稿，不要实际发送。交给独立审核席打开新版核对，再由首席在本对话交回最终调度表和对应版本的审核记录。

## English

The North Building closes from 09:00 until 10:00 and reopens at exactly 10:00. Keep all jobs at their original locations. Preserve unaffected bookings.

Have production save a new revision with a dispatch sheet ready for use. For each job give the start and end, location, and why its booking was retained or changed. Draft notices for the affected sites; do not send them. Have a separate reviewer open and inspect the new revision. The lead should return the final schedule and its matching review in this conversation.
