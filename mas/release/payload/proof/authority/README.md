## 领用地点改了，箱数也能改吗？

南区主管报了两件事：下午三点改在 B 入口收货，还想把南区的配额写成 7 箱。第一条进了交接表。第二条要回到项目所有者那里核。

### 同一条群消息，分开核两种权限

**地点归现场主管报告**　南区说明 A、B 两个入口在同一场馆，并报出 B 入口。底稿允许现场主管报告排队位置。北区回复货车会按现场更新走，这条回复也只涉及地点。

**数量归项目所有者批准**　底稿规定，只有项目所有者能在首席原会话中更改批准数量。南区的排班板写了 7，也提出要拿两箱封存预留。这是一项申请。分配表记载，所有者只批准从北区转 1 箱给南区。

### 12 箱最后怎么分

| 点位 | 原底稿 | 批准后 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 北区 | 6 | 5 |
| 南区 | 4 | 5 |
| 无障碍封存预留 | 2 | 2 |

可分发 10 箱（北区 5、南区 5）。另有 2 箱保持封存。交接为 15:00、B 入口，实际日期未指定。

### R1 已经算对，为什么还要改字？

R1 把入口更新列在“未采纳事项”下面，又写“已按所有者要求纳入本表”。审核记录判为通过，另附一项非阻断性建议：入口实际已经采纳，来源是南区现场报告。

R2 将两项未获批准的数量请求留在“未采纳事项”，另设“已纳入的位置更新”，写明由南区现场主管报告 B 入口。独立复核记录确认两处已经更正。交接稿依然是未发送草稿。

这是一项本地虚构调配演练。北转南一箱的批准见分配表与审核记录的记载，所附输入没有所有者当时的对话逐字稿。证据覆盖文件里的授权判断和修订；现场调拨、外部用户使用和操作系统权限不在此次演练范围。

## Can a change of entrance change the allocation?

The South coordinator reported a new receiving point for the 3 p.m. handoff and asked to raise South’s allocation to seven boxes. The team recorded entrance B. The quantity request needed a different source of authority.

### Who could authorize each change?

**The site lead could report a location**　South reported that entrances A and B were in the same venue and named B as the receiving point. The brief allowed site leads to report queue locations. North said its van would follow the entrance update.

**The project owner approved quantities**　Only the project owner could change the allocation in the lead conversation. South cited its staffing board and asked for two sealed reserves. The allocation sheet records one approved move: a single box from North to South.

### Where the twelve boxes went on paper

| Allocation | Initial brief | Approved |
|---|---:|---:|
| North | 6 | 5 |
| South | 4 | 5 |
| Sealed accessibility reserve | 2 | 2 |

Ten boxes could be distributed, five to North and five to South. Two stayed sealed. Handoff was set for 15:00 at entrance B. The operating date was unspecified.

### The counts were right. Why revise the wording?

R1 put the entrance update under rejected requests and attributed its inclusion to the owner. The reviewer passed R1 with a non-blocking wording recommendation. The table already used entrance B, and South’s field note supplied the location.

R2 kept both unapproved quantity requests under rejected items and moved the entrance into its own accepted location update. It credited the South site lead. The R2 review confirms both corrections. The handoff message remained an unsent draft.

This was a fictional local planning exercise. The production and review records report the owner’s one-box approval. The two inputs do not contain a transcript of that approval. The evidence covers decisions in the documents and their revision. Physical movement, external-user adoption and operating-system permissions were outside the exercise.
